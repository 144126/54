THIS AGREEMENT is made this ................ day of ...................., 2026.
BETWEEN
MRS EDEM HOGAN of PW, Maroko Road, Kubwa District, Abuja (hereinafter referred to as “the Appointor”) which expression shall where the context so admits include its successors-in-title and assigns, of the one part;
AND
JIDE TAIWO AND COMPANY of No. 70, Abidjan Street, Wuse Zone 3, Abuja (hereinafter referred to as “the Appointee”) which expression shall where the context so admits include its successors-in-title and assigns, of the other part.
WHEREAS
The Appointor is the bona fide owner of the detached building located at PW, Maroko Road, Kubwa District, Abuja (hereinafter referred to as
“the Property”).
The Appointor desires to engage the services of the Appointee for the letting and management of the Property, and both parties have agreed to formalize their relationship on the terms and conditions contained in this Agreement.

NOW THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:
1. APPOINTMENT
- The Appointor hereby appoints the Appointee as her exclusive agent for the letting and management of the Property for the Term of this Agreement.
- The Appointee accepts the appointment and agrees to perform the services professionally, diligently, and in accordance with the highest standards of the Estate Surveying and Valuation profession.
2. DURATION OF THE AGREEMENT
- This Agreement shall commence on the date of execution and shall continue for an initial term of 2 (two) years (“the Initial Term”).
- The Agreement may be renewable for a further term(s) upon mutual agreement of the parties, subject to review of fees and terms.
3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE APPOINTEE
The Appointee hereby covenant with the appointor as follows:
- To oversee the property and the tenant occupying same.
- To advise her on the best rent accruable on the property.
- To lease out the property to an approved prospective tenant at an agreed rent terms by the Appointor.
- To collect due rent from the tenant as and when due.
- To carry out routine inspection on the property, appraise its status and update the Appointor.
- To carry out instruction of the Appointor as regard repairs.

- On collection of rent, pay same into Appointor’s account as provided by the Appointor not later than 48hours of such collections except otherwise instructed by the appointer.

1. OBLIGATIONS OF THE APPOINTOR
The Appointor shall:
- Provide the Appointee with all necessary documents, information, and access to the Property to enable performance of its duties.
- Be responsible for the payment of the agreed Letting Management Fee to the Appointee, which is put at 10 percent (10%) of the rental value excluding the first year rent.
- Bear the cost of major repairs and capital improvements
- Be responsible for legal costs in proceedings in respect of the property.
- Be Responsible for payments of ground rent, while the tenants shall be responsible for the payments of tenement rates and other AMAC charges.
5. Reporting and Transparency
- Maintain proper books of account for all transactions.
- Submit quarterly reports on tenancy status, collections, expenses, and Property condition.
- Submit annual comprehensive reports. 10% as Management Fee of the rent which is to be paid by the Appointor.
6. Insurance: Advise on and ensure adequate insurance cover for the Property, where instructed by the Appointor.
7. FEES AND PAYMENTS
- The Appointee shall be entitled to 10% of the rent as management fee.
- Management fee shall be deducted from the rent before remittance to the appointor.
8. TERMINATION
- Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving six (6) months’ written notice to the other party.
- Either party may terminate immediately by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 14 days of receiving notice of the breach.
- Upon termination, the Appointee shall hand over all documents, records, keys, and funds within 14 days and provide a final account.
9. INDEMNITY
- The Appointee shall indemnify the Appointor against all losses, claims, and liabilities arising from the Appointee’s negligence, misconduct, or breach of this Agreement.
- The Appointor shall indemnify the Appointee against losses arising from the Appointor’s breach or provision of false information.
10. CONFIDENTIALITY: Both parties shall keep confidential all information relating to the other party and the Property and shall not disclose it without prior written consent, except as required by law. This obligation shall survive the termination of this Agreement.
11. FORCE MAJEURE: Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations under this Agreement to the extent that such failure or delay is caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, riots, strikes, government regulations or natural disasters, provided prompt written notice is given.
