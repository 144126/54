To the elders and evangelists
The church of Christ
Efab Junction, Kado
Abuja


Thank you for your invitation with regards to my concerns over the situation in our congregation. I am to God for the grace over your lives as you continue to  serve in His vineyard and that by extension of that Grace we are able to share the benefits of Gods love all continue to  submitting this brief report to provide a written record of some of the matters that have occurred. I may not be able to adequately explain everything during our meeting, so I have summarized the principal issues below.

My intention is not to create division or attack anyone personally. I am bringing these matters before you because I desire peace, fairness, and a proper resolution consistent with the Scriptures and the principles of the Church.

1. Background

Our congregation was established from [name of congregation], which currently has elders. Because our local congregation does not have its own elders, I understand that the local congregation is autonomous. However, I am grateful that the elders of the parent congregation have agreed to hear and consider these concerns.

2. Disagreement concerning administrative responsibilities

At various times, I raised concerns about the preacher being involved in, or exercising control over, administrative matters that I believed should not fall under his responsibility as the preacher.

When this was brought to his attention, his response was not what I expected from a brother seeking peaceful cooperation. This disagreement appears to have subsequently affected my relationship with him and, in my view, the way I and my children have been treated in the congregation.

3. Disfellowshipping of myself and two of my children

In [month/year], myself and two of my children were disfellowshipped.

I believe there are important circumstances surrounding this action that I would like the elders to hear and examine carefully. I am prepared to provide the details, including what was said and done before and after the action, and the persons who were involved or witnessed relevant events.

4. Attempts to have us leave the congregation

Following our disfellowshipping, I became aware of efforts to make our continued presence in the congregation difficult.

Some members of the other congregation subsequently came and appealed to the preacher for peaceful coexistence. However, I was told that he preferred that we leave the congregation and worship elsewhere.

Despite this, we chose to continue worshipping with the congregation because we believed we had a right to worship there and did not want the disagreement to result in our leaving.

5. Treatment of members who disagree with him

There have been several occasions where I have observed or experienced what I consider to be hostile or discouraging treatment when someone disagrees with the preacher or questions something he has done.

In my own case, I have experienced [briefly state specific examples]. On some occasions, matters concerning me have also been mentioned from the pulpit in a manner that I felt was directed toward me or intended to cast me in a negative light.

I would be willing to provide specific examples and identify witnesses where applicable.

6. Removal from the Finance Committee

I was previously a member of the Finance Committee. I was subsequently removed from the committee by the preacher.

I understood the Finance Committee to be a congregational committee rather than something that should be controlled by the preacher personally. I therefore became concerned about the manner in which the committee was being administered.

More recently, when members were invited to select committees, I indicated that I wished to return to the Finance Committee. I was told that I could not be a member of that committee.

This raised further concerns for me, particularly in light of the earlier disagreement concerning the preacher's involvement in financial administration.

7. Involvement with the Finance WhatsApp platform

I also became aware that the preacher was serving as an administrator of the Finance Committee's WhatsApp platform.

I questioned whether this was appropriate given his role as preacher and the distinction between preaching responsibilities and the congregation's financial administration. My concern was that financial matters should be handled by the brethren appointed to that responsibility rather than being subject to unnecessary control by the preacher.

8. Removal from the congregational WhatsApp platform

I and my children were also removed from the congregation's WhatsApp platform.

I recognize that membership of a WhatsApp group is not itself a spiritual matter. I mention it only as part of the broader pattern of events, because it contributed to my concern that we were being deliberately excluded from the normal life and communication of the congregation.

9. My decision to worship elsewhere temporarily

Because the situation had become increasingly uncomfortable, I attended worship with the other congregation last Sunday and brought these concerns to the attention of the elders.

I am not seeking to create a conflict between the two congregations. Rather, I am asking for an opportunity for these matters to be examined fairly, with all sides heard, so that there can be peace and proper order among brethren.

10. What I am asking from the elders

I respectfully ask the elders to:

Hear my account of these events and allow the relevant brethren to give their accounts as well.

Examine the circumstances surrounding the disfellowshipping of myself and my two children.

Consider whether the preacher's involvement in administrative and financial affairs is appropriate.

Consider the manner in which disagreements and disciplinary matters have been handled.

Help determine how these matters can be resolved peacefully and scripturally.

Help establish a healthy working arrangement for the congregation so that no individual unnecessarily controls matters that belong to the congregation or its appointed committees.

I am not presenting this report because I want the preacher removed or because I desire personal vindication. My primary desire is for truth, peace, fairness, and a congregation where brethren can worship and work together without intimidation, personal conflicts, or unnecessary division.

I am willing to provide further details, dates, messages, names of witnesses, and other relevant information where necessary.

Thank you for taking the time to consider these concerns. I pray that our meeting will help bring about a peaceful and scriptural resolution.

Respectfully,
