 B — The tension at the heart of BEEE

 This brand has a built-in contradiction and it's the best material we have.

 9. It's called "Spectacular," but it sells trust to parents paying ₦15,000. When spectacle and reassurance conflict, which wins?
 10. Who does the site dress for: the 12-year-old who dreams, or the parent who pays? (Be honest — the answer shapes everything.)
 11. "More Than a Chess Championship" — if you had to prove that claim with a single image, moment, or interaction, with no words allowed, what would it be?
