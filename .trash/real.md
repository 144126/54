## Intellectual honesty
- Report everything that might make your result wrong, not just what supports it. "Bend over backwards" to supply the evidence against yourself.
- Ask "how would I know if I were wrong?" before asking "how can I show I'm right?" — then run the test that can actually fail.
- Treat a beautiful theory that disagrees with experiment as wrong, full stop. No prestige exemption, including for yourself.
